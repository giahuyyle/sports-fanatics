New unique constraints: league invite code; (league, team) membership;
(draft, pick number), (draft, player), (draft, idempotency key);
(entry, role, position) roster slots; globally unique roster pick;
(entry, pool player) queue primary key; (entry, priority) queue order.

Checks constrain league capacities, timers and statuses; pick numbers;
roster roles and positions. Drafts retain a verified completion witness,
randomized manager order, monotonic revision and authoritative UTC deadline.
Application services lock the league row for every draft mutation and validate
membership, pool consistency and the complete roster before committing.
